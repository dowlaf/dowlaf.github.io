Today was very physically challenging. My feet are the worst they have been pain wise, and my diarrhea has returned with a true vengeance. Because of one of the medications I’m taking my feet are extremely dry and have deep cracks through all the layers of skin, so every step is painful. To maintain our timeline after yesterday’s short day, we opted to walk 34 miles, from the Peter Grub Hut at Mile 1161.9 all the way to Sierra City, Mile 1196.5. 

We were walking by 4 AM and after my first bout of diarrhea, I was soon separated from the group, not to be seen until just a bit before lunch. The terrain for the morning was a series of up-and-down each 500 to 1000 feet of gain with about the same loss. We were able to do all of these by about noon, thus covering the first 18 or so miles, just leaving a 20 mile descent into Sierra City. Though my feet were killing me. The descent was fairly easy, though it did bring with it much higher air temperatures as we lost significant elevation.

We were able to quickly hitch into the cute little downtown Sierra city, where we went to the country store for burger, fries, and a milkshake. We ended up sleeping on the front porch like true bums.
