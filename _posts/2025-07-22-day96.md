Today we did a half day out of Bend, walking fifteen miles to end at Mile 1968.1. We barely made it out, as Bend was so awesome that we all felt like we could stay for weeks. 

After a delicious breakfast at Jackson’s Corner, I got two cavities filled. The dentist and his assistant were very silly, maintaining a meandering and variegated conversation for the entire length of the procedure. 

![1](/images/IMG_7148.jpeg)

I caught up with the gang at Miller’s Landing, a beautiful park on the Deschutes River. We relaxed for a time until gaining the courage to walk. We left the trailhead at 2:00. The walking was easy and the landscape was certainly the prettiest I have walked through in Oregon. We had many stunning views of the South and Middle Sisters while walking through meadows filled with fragrant wildflowers. Great day.
