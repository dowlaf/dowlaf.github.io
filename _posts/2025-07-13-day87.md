Because of the insane heat wave in Southern Oregon (it was 100! Fahrenheit at 5 pm!) and we wanted to sleep in and enjoy our house to the fullest extent, we decided to begin in the evening and hike into the night. We spent our day well—it began with a huge breakfast of eggs, bacon, French toast, fruit, berries, yogurt, and leftover pie. When Roadrunner’s parents dropped us off at Lithia Park, I immediately ate my pint of Ben and Jerry’s, caught up on the blog, and took a nice nap. 

![1](/images/IMG_7016.jpeg)

I ate another fried chicken sandwich from bird and rye while making phone calls, munched some leftover watermelon and peaches, and soon enough it was 5 pm and time to hit the trail. We hiked a very chill eleven miles, with lots of shade and an insanely pretty sunset. Dinner by headlamp isn’t a treat I have had in awhile, and the mosquitos made me inhale my rice, beans, avocado, and Fritos with little of the usual pleasure. I have no idea what tomorrow hold and I’m excited!
